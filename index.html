FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this book beginner-friendly?
Absolutely. It's written specifically for complete beginners with zero prior experience - plain language, step-by-step instructions, and a clear roadmap from your first idea to your first sale.
Do I need technical skills?
No. Most products in the book are built with free, beginner-friendly tools like Canva, Google Docs and Notion. For the few technical products, no-code options and simple alternatives are provided.
Can I start without money?
Yes. Most digital products cost between $0 and $100 to create using free or low-cost tools, and many of the selling platforms covered are free to join and only charge a small fee per sale.
Which digital products should I create first?
The book recommends starting with one product type suited to your skills - often an ebook, template or printable - validating demand first, then expanding into a portfolio over time.
Does the book include AI tools?
Yes. It includes AI-friendly strategies and a master list of 40+ tools and platforms to help you create products and market them faster and more efficiently.
Where can I sell my products?
You'll learn how to sell on Etsy, Gumroad, Payhip, Creative Market, Teachable, Amazon KDP and more - with guidance on which platform fits each product type and how to use platform SEO.
Is this suitable outside my country?
Yes. Digital products sell globally and the strategies work wherever you live. The platforms covered serve international sellers and buyers, so a single product can earn worldwide.
Will I receive updates?
The book is written to stay practical and current. Keep an eye on your purchase platform for any updated editions and additional resources released by the author.
